Mission

Build things that work when it matters. That has meant different things over the years — a control loop that holds steady in a plant, a status report that has to be right the first time in a command post, and now a mobile game that thousands of rounds a day run through without a hitch. I like work where “close enough” isn’t.

About me

I’m an instrumentation and calibration field technician based in Westerville, Ohio. Most of my career has been spent around industrial process equipment — temperature, pressure, and flow instruments that plants depend on — calibrating them, troubleshooting them, and keeping them inside spec.

I also serve as a Staff Sergeant and Command Post Controller in the Ohio Air National Guard, where I handle tactical communications and operational reporting during drills and active duty. It taught me how to stay useful when things get loud.

In 2026 I founded Chaotic Game Studios LLC and taught myself mobile game development the practical way: by shipping. Our first title, Strike-Command: Idle Esports, is live on Google Play — I built the game, the backend, and the release pipeline, and I run the studio’s business side too. I’m currently finishing my degree at DeVry University.

What I bring

  • Clear communication under pressure — command-post reporting and coordination
  • Self-taught software delivery — game client, backend services, store publishing
  • Small-business ownership — LLC formation, banking, taxes, vendor accounts
