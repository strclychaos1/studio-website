About me

I’m a precision measurement and instrumentation technician based in Westerville, Ohio, with 12 years in the trade — currently an Electrical & Instrumentation Technician at FCX Performance. I started in the U.S. Air Force’s metrology career field (2P0X1 — Precision Measurement Equipment Laboratory), spending six years on active duty (November 2013 – November 2019) and leaving as a 7-level Craftsman. After that came four years of commercial calibration work, including time at the Air Force Primary Standards Laboratory — the lab the rest of the Air Force’s measurements trace back to.

I still serve as a Staff Sergeant and Command Post Controller in the Ohio Air National Guard, handling tactical communications and operational reporting during drills and active duty. It taught me how to stay useful when things get loud.

In 2026 I founded Chaotic Game Studios LLC and taught myself mobile game development the practical way: by shipping. Our first title, Strike-Command: Idle Esports, is live on Google Play — I built the game, the backend, and the release pipeline, and I run the studio’s business side too. I’m currently finishing my degree at DeVry University.
