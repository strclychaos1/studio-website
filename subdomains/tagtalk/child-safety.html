Child Safety Standards

Last updated: August 2026

Our commitment

Chaotic Game Studios LLC has a zero-tolerance policy for child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE) across every app we publish, including TagTalk and Strike-Command: Idle Scrims. Any content that sexualizes minors, exploits children, or facilitates child abuse is strictly prohibited and will be removed immediately. Accounts involved in such activity are permanently banned.

Apps covered by these standards

  • TagTalk — a social messaging app with user-generated content (package com.chaoticgamestudios.tagtalk).
  • Strike-Command: Idle Scrims — an idle esports team-manager game (package com.chaoticgamestudios.strikecommand).

These standards apply to every current and future Chaotic Game Studios title.

Age policy

Our apps are not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If we learn that an account belongs to a child under 13, we will delete it and its associated data.

How we prevent and detect abuse

  • Human moderation (TagTalk) — all user-uploaded photos are reviewed by a moderator before they are visible to other users.
  • In-app reporting (TagTalk) — every message can be reported by any user with a single tap. Reports are reviewed by our moderation team.
  • Account accountability — even "anonymous" content is internally linked to the author's account, so abusive users can be identified and banned.
  • Ownership verification (TagTalk) — plate ownership disputes are settled with vehicle registration review, deterring impersonation and harassment.
  • No user-to-user contact (Strike-Command) — the game has no chat, messaging, or user-generated content. The only user-supplied text is a player name shown on leaderboards, which our staff can review and reset via our admin tools.
  • Support tickets (all apps) — every app includes an in-app support ticket system staffed by our team, where safety concerns can be raised directly.

How to report child safety concerns

If you encounter any content or behavior that may involve the exploitation of a child, report it immediately:

  • Use the Report button on any message in TagTalk, or the Support ticket system in any of our apps.
  • Email us at contact@chaoticgamestudios.com with as much detail as possible. Child safety reports are prioritized.

Where required by law, we report apparent child sexual abuse material to the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC) via the CyberTipline, and we cooperate with law enforcement.

Enforcement

  • CSAE content is removed immediately upon detection or report.
  • Accounts posting or soliciting such content are permanently banned.
  • We preserve and share evidence with authorities when legally required.

Compliance

We comply with all applicable child safety laws and regulations, and we review these standards regularly to keep them aligned with Google Play's Child Safety Standards policy and industry best practices.

Contact

Child safety contact: contact@chaoticgamestudios.com. We aim to respond to child safety reports within 24 hours.
