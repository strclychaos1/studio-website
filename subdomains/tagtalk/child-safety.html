Child Safety Standards

Last updated: July 2026

Our commitment

TagTalk has a zero-tolerance policy for child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE). Any content that sexualizes minors, exploits children, or facilitates child abuse is strictly prohibited and will be removed immediately. Accounts involved in such activity are permanently banned.

Age policy

TagTalk is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If we learn that an account belongs to a child under 13, we will delete it and its associated data.

How we prevent and detect abuse

  • Human moderation — all user-uploaded photos are reviewed by a moderator before they are visible to other users.
  • In-app reporting — every message can be reported by any user with a single tap. Reports are reviewed by our moderation team.
  • Account accountability — even "anonymous" messages are internally linked to the author's account, so abusive users can be identified and banned.
  • Ownership verification — plate ownership disputes are settled with vehicle registration review, deterring impersonation and harassment.

How to report child safety concerns

If you encounter any content or behavior that may involve the exploitation of a child, report it immediately:

  • Use the Report button on any message in the app.
  • Email us at support@tagtalk.chaoticgamestudios.com with as much detail as possible. Child safety reports are prioritized.

Where required by law, we report apparent child sexual abuse material to the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC) via the CyberTipline, and we cooperate with law enforcement.

Enforcement

  • CSAE content is removed immediately upon detection or report.
  • Accounts posting or soliciting such content are permanently banned.
  • We preserve and share evidence with authorities when legally required.

Contact

Child safety officer contact: support@tagtalk.chaoticgamestudios.com. We aim to respond to child safety reports within 24 hours.
