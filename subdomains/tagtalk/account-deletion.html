Account & Data Deletion

You have the right to request deletion of your TagTalk account and the personal data associated with it. This page explains exactly how to request it, what happens next, and what is kept for legal or fraud‑prevention reasons.

Last updated: June 2026

Request deletion in three steps

  1. Send us an email From the email address associated with your TagTalk account, send a message to support@tagtalk.chaoticgamestudios.com. Use the button below to open your mail app with a pre‑filled request.
  2. We verify your identity To protect you from someone else deleting your account, we confirm that the request came from the account's registered email. If we can't verify, we'll reply and ask for a simple confirmation.
  3. We delete your data within 30 days Once verified, we permanently delete your account, vehicles, and outgoing messages from our production database. You'll receive an email confirmation when it's done.
Email deletion request

What gets deleted

The following data is permanently removed when your request is completed:

Data typeWhat happens
Google Sign-In identity (name, email, profile picture) Deleted
Vehicles linked to your garage (plate, state, make, model, color, photos) Deleted
Messages, ratings, and tags you sent to other plates Deleted
Plate ownership claims you submitted (including registration photos) Deleted
Purchase history (Google Play tokens, unlock status) Deleted
Login sessions Deleted

What is kept, and why

For legal, safety, and fraud‑prevention reasons, a small amount of data may be retained after account deletion:

Data typeRetention
Records of abuse reports or moderator actions taken against you (anonymized where possible) Kept up to 2 years
Anonymized transaction records required by tax / accounting laws (order ID, amount, date — no personal identifiers) Kept up to 7 years
Server backups containing deleted data (rotated automatically) Up to 30 days
Note: Google Play manages your subscription billing history separately. Cancelling your subscription and requesting deletion here does not affect refund eligibility — for refunds, contact Google Play support directly at support.google.com/googleplay.

Delete individual data without closing your account

If you only want to remove specific items, you don't need to close your account:

  • Delete a vehicle: open the app → Garage tab → tap the vehicle → Delete.
  • Cancel your subscription: open Google Play Store → menu → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions → TagTalk → Cancel.
  • Reset ad personalization: Android Settings → Google → Ads → Delete advertising ID.
  • Withdraw a claim you submitted: email us — we'll remove the pending claim and its registration photo.

How long does the process take?

  • Same day: we acknowledge receipt of your request.
  • Within 7 days: we verify identity (if not already confirmed) and begin deletion.
  • Within 30 days: all data is deleted from active systems.
  • Within 30 days after that: all data is purged from rotating backups.

Contact

Questions or concerns about deletion? Reach us at support@tagtalk.chaoticgamestudios.com. We aim to respond within one business day.
