Frequently asked
What is TagTalk?

TagTalk lets you leave a message, rating, or tag on any license plate you spot. If someone claims that plate, they'll see everything the community has said about their driving.

How do I add my own plate?

Open the Garage tab and tap the + button. Enter your plate number, state, and vehicle details. To unlock the messages left on it, tap Unlock and choose Lifetime or Monthly.

Someone else registered my plate. What do I do?

Tap Submit a Claim on the plate result screen and attach a photo of your vehicle registration. A moderator will review and transfer the plate to you — typically within 24–48 hours.

Why can't I read messages left on my own plate?

Reading incoming messages requires a one-time Lifetime unlock ($19.99) or a Monthly subscription ($2.99). You can send messages to other plates for free.

How does anonymous mode work?

When you send a message anonymously, your name is hidden from the recipient and everyone else. We still record who sent it internally, so we can moderate abuse. Please use it kindly.

Can I rate the same plate multiple times?

No — each user can leave only one review per plate. You also can't rate your own plate.

I purchased on another device. How do I restore my unlock?

Open the Unlock screen and tap "Restore purchases" at the bottom. Make sure you're signed into the same Google account you originally purchased with.

How do I cancel my Monthly subscription?

Cancel any time from the Google Play Store → Menu → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions → TagTalk. You keep access until the end of the current billing period.

How do I report an abusive message?

Every message in your inbox has a report button (… menu). Tap it, choose a reason, and our moderators will review the report.

How do I delete my account?

Email us at support@tagtalk.chaoticgamestudios.com from the address associated with your account, or follow the steps on our Account & Data Deletion page. We'll delete your account, vehicles, and outgoing messages within 30 days.

Still stuck?

Reach us directly at support@tagtalk.chaoticgamestudios.com. We aim to respond within one business day.
