  • Purchases (Lifetime unlock, Monthly subscription) are billed by Google Play and subject to Google Play's terms and refund policies.

  • You can delete your account at any time — see Account & Data Deletion.

Who we are

TagTalk is developed by Chaotic Game Studios. This policy explains what personal information we collect from you when you use the TagTalk mobile app, how we use it, and the choices you have.

Information we collect

  • Account info — your name, email address, and profile picture provided by Google Sign‑In.
  • Vehicle info you enter — license plate, state, make, model, color, and any photos you upload.
  • Messages, tags, and ratings you leave on other plates.
  • Purchase records — Google Play purchase tokens used to verify your unlock status. We never see or store your payment card.
  • Basic technical data — device model, OS version, and crash logs, used only to keep the app stable.
  • Advertising identifiers — a resettable Google Advertising ID, used by Google AdMob to serve banner ads to non‑paying users.

How we use your information

  • To operate the core service (searching plates, storing your garage, delivering messages).
  • To verify purchases with Google Play and unlock your inbox.
  • To moderate abusive content and enforce our community rules.
  • To respond to your support requests.
  • To display ads via Google AdMob to users who have not purchased an unlock.

Anonymous messages

When you choose to send a message anonymously, your name is hidden from the recipient and from other users. However, we always record who you are internally so we can moderate abusive content and comply with law enforcement requests when legally required.

Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We share limited data only with:

  • Google (for Sign‑In, Play Billing, and AdMob) — governed by Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Our hosting providers (IONOS — app servers; MongoDB Atlas — database) — used strictly to run the app.
  • Law enforcement, when required by a valid legal request.

Advertising

TagTalk shows banner ads to users on the free tier via Google AdMob. AdMob uses your device's Advertising ID and general device data to serve relevant ads. You can reset or opt out of personalized ads at any time in your device's Google settings under Ads → Delete Advertising ID. Paying users (Lifetime or active Monthly subscription) do not see any ads.

Your choices

  • Delete a vehicle at any time from your Garage — associated messages remain but are unlinked.
  • Request full account deletion by emailing us at support@tagtalk.chaoticgamestudios.com. We will remove your account, vehicles, and outgoing messages within 30 days.
  • Manage subscription renewal directly in the Google Play Store.
  • Reset your Advertising ID in your device's Google settings to limit ad personalization.

Data retention

We keep your data for as long as your account is active. If you request deletion we remove personal data within 30 days, except where a longer retention is required for legal, tax, or fraud‑prevention purposes.

Children

TagTalk is not directed to children under 13. If you believe we have collected information from a child, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes

We may update this policy occasionally. Material changes will be surfaced in the app. Continued use of TagTalk after changes take effect means you accept the updated policy.

Contact

Questions or data requests? Reach us at support@tagtalk.chaoticgamestudios.com.
