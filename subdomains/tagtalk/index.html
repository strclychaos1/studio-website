How it works

From download to your first note in under a minute.

  1. Sign in with Google One tap, no passwords to remember.
  2. Add your vehicles Plate, state, make, model, and an optional photo — that's your garage.
  3. Search a plate or leave a note Say thanks for the parking spot save, or warn the next driver about a habitual lane-cutter.

A community with guardrails

Anonymous doesn't mean unaccountable.

Every message can be reported and is reviewed by human moderators. Anonymous senders are hidden from other users, but never from our moderation team. Ownership disputes are settled with vehicle registration, and abuse gets accounts banned — not argued with.
