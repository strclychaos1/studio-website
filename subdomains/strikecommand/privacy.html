Who we are

Strike-Command: Idle Scrims is developed by Chaotic Game Studios LLC. This policy explains what information the game collects, how it is used, and the choices you have. The short version: the game runs on your device and we operate no game servers. We never see your save file, your team, or how you play.

Information we collect

  • Game progress — stored locally on your device only. If you enable cloud save, a copy is written to your own Google Drive (hidden app data folder) — we cannot access it.
  • Account info (optional) — if you choose to sign in with Google for cloud save, the game receives your name, email address, and profile picture from Google Sign-In, used only to display who is signed in.
  • Purchase records — Google Play purchase tokens used to verify your purchases and restore them on reinstall. We never see or store your payment card.
  • Advertising identifiers — a resettable Google Advertising ID, used by Google AdMob to serve ads.
  • Basic technical data — device model, OS version, and crash/diagnostic data collected by Google Play and AdMob, used only to keep the game stable.
  • Ranked play data (optional) — if you sign in and join the hosted world ranking, we store your team name, color, country, team power, league results, and your Google account ID on our server so your team can appear on the shared leaderboard.

How we use your information

  • To save and restore your game progress (locally, and to your own Google Drive if you enable cloud save).
  • To verify and restore purchases with Google Play.
  • To display ads via Google AdMob (rewarded ads you choose to watch, and occasional interstitials).
  • To operate the hosted world ranking — showing your team on a shared leaderboard alongside other players and simulated teams.
  • To respond to support requests.

Advertising

The game shows ads via Google AdMob. AdMob uses your device's Advertising ID and general device data (such as approximate location derived from IP) to serve and measure ads. Where required (EEA/UK), the game asks for your consent choices on first launch via Google's consent platform, and you can change them at any time in the game settings. You can also reset or delete your Advertising ID in your device's Google settings under Ads → Delete Advertising ID. The one-time No Ads purchase removes all ads permanently.

Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. Data is shared only with:

  • Google (Sign-In, Drive backup, Play Billing, AdMob, and Play crash reporting) — governed by Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Law enforcement, when required by a valid legal request.

We run no analytics or tracking of our own. Ranked play data is stored on a server we operate (hosted by IONOS) and is used only to run the leaderboard; it is never sold or shared with advertisers. All other game data stays on your device.

Your choices

  • Delete local data: clear the app's storage or uninstall it — your local save is gone with it.
  • Delete cloud save: remove the game's data from your Google Drive app settings, or sign out in the game.
  • Remove ads: purchase the No Ads unlock, or reset your Advertising ID to limit personalization.
  • Refunds: managed through Google Play's refund process.
  • Questions or data requests? Email support@strikecommand.chaoticgamestudios.com.

Data retention

Because game data lives on your device (and optionally in your own Google Drive), retention is under your control — deleting the app or the Drive backup removes it. Ranked play data is kept on our server while your team is registered and removed within 30 days of a deletion request. Purchase verification records are retained by Google Play per their policies.

Children

Strike-Command: Idle Scrims is not directed to children under 13, and ads served to users who indicate they are under the age of consent are restricted accordingly through Google's consent tools. If you believe a child has provided us information, contact us and we will address it.
