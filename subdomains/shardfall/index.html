SHARDFALL

A Medieval Fantasy Strategy Realm

Raise your keep from the wilds, recruit heroes to rule and lead your armies, conquer valleys, scout rival lords, and wage war across a persistent shard — one castle, one banner, no paywalls.

In development — Android & iOS apps coming

The Realm Awaits

Everything runs on a living shard that never sleeps — even when you do.

Raise Your Keep

Ten buildings from Farm to Castle Wall, each with 20 levels. A Warehouse hides your riches from raiders.

Recruit Heroes

Heroes of the Tavern rule as mayor — boosting production — or lead your marches and scout missions into battle.

Research & Muster

The Academy unlocks Masonry, Warfare, Supply Lines and Artisanship. Spearmen, Archers, Knights and Scouts await your command.

Conquer Valleys

500×500 tiles of living world. Wrest valleys from their garrisons for production, and raze barbarian camps for loot.

Scout & Raid

Send scouts to unveil enemy keeps — if their own scouts don't intercept yours. Then march, plunder, and vanish.

Swear Alliances

Found or join an alliance, coordinate in private chat, and teleport your keep across the shard to fight side by side.
